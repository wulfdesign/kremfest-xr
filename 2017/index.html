KremFest XR 2017 Genesis

During the inaugural 2017 edition of KremFest, virtual reality was first introduced to the Kremwerk Complex, powered by Kinetoscope VR.

The Genesis of VR at KremFest

In September 2017, as Kremwerk launched the very first KremFest multi-room electronic music festival, an experimental virtual reality installation was introduced on-site powered by Kinetoscope VR. Attendees were able to don headsets and experience initial 360° and interactive cinema.

Following this 2017 pilot, curation and production were officially handed over to Larry James (VRMakerDome / Wulf Design) and Julia Jackson (UpLiftVR Studios) starting with the landmark 2018 edition, establishing an international juried showcase with room-scale headliners, worldwide submissions, and technical hardware wrangling.
