Parietal Lab Generative Spatial Suite

Grant Hinkson (Parietal Lab / Coliseum of Art)
CONNECTOME
CONSTELLATIONS
Project Hydra
🔮 MOST SURREAL SPATIAL LINEUP

Awarded for the most mind-expanding and meditative sensory spatial suite of the festival across three transcendent works: CONNECTOME (synaptic neural pathways), CONSTELLATIONS: Touch the Stars (celestial IAU starlight tracing), and Project Hydra: Iterations (generative architectural liminal forms). Quietly tracing cosmic starlight while club subwoofers vibrated the room created an unforgettable contemplative contrast, continuously mirrored to lounge wall displays.
