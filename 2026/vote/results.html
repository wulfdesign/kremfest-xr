🎖️ Official Curatorial Honors & Special Lineup Accolades

Alongside the top attendee floor requests on the podium, KremFest XR officially honors the visionary creators whose technical innovation, soundstage synergy, and boundary-pushing artistry defined the 2026 showcase:

🎧 VIP SPATIAL AUDIO SPOTLIGHT

djay: DJ in VR & Spatial Audio

Algoriddim (Karim Morsy & Richard)

Awarded for pioneering mixed reality turntablism on Meta Quest 3. Spinning virtual vinyl inside XR passthrough while real-world club subwoofers thudded through the venue blended seamlessly with djay's spatial decks, creating an unforgettable acoustic-spatial resonance paired with 1080p wall-screen casting.

📊 Floor Standing: #4 Most Requested Experience • 5 votes (6.9%)

🎖️ CURATORIAL HONORABLE MENTION & DEEP EXPLORATION FAVORITE

Firedrake VR: The Wizard's Warren Demo

Jon (Hues GFX)

Awarded for evocative dark fantasy world-building, ancient stone dungeon crawl pacing, and tactile glowing potion bottles. A midnight highlight of the showcase, featuring a standout 15-minute dungeon exploration walkthrough by festival director DJ Julia Camp.

📊 Floor Standing: #5 Most Requested Experience • 4 votes (5.6%)
🎴 View Lineup Profile → 🌿 Laurels
⚡ VIP HIGH-ENERGY DANCE ACCLAIM

Trip the Light: Let's Dance!

Patrick Ascolese (Red Nexus Games & Dark Arts Software)

Awarded for high-energy kinetic rhythm agency and physical dancefloor immersion. Moving to high-tempo rhythmic beats inside the headset while the nightclub's subwoofers pumped through the floor delivered an electric physical sync that had attendees grooving.

📊 Floor Standing: #6 Most Requested Experience • 4 votes (5.6%)

Project Hydra

Parietal Lab Generative Spatial Suite

Grant Hinkson (Parietal Lab / Coliseum of Art)

Awarded for the most mind-expanding and meditative sensory spatial suite of the festival across three transcendent works: CONNECTOME (synaptic neural pathways), CONSTELLATIONS: Touch the Stars (celestial IAU starlight tracing), and Project Hydra: Iterations (generative architectural liminal forms). Quietly tracing cosmic starlight while club subwoofers vibrated the room created an unforgettable contemplative contrast, continuously mirrored to lounge wall displays.

📊 Floor Standing: 11 Combined Requests across 3 Works • CONNECTOME (4), CONSTELLATIONS (4), Project Hydra (3)

We Are Dead Animals

Maarten Isaäk de Heer (Tote Tiere Maarten)

Awarded for bold, provocative stop-motion zoetrope cinema. Featured during late-night midnight haunt sets, where its surreal animation world captivated festival attendees and offered lounge patrons a striking artistic encounter on venue spectator monitors.
