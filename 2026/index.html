5th Edition KremFest XR Showcase

Submissions are open for the 5th edition of KREMFEST XR (and 4th curated by VRMakerDome & UpLiftVR Studios) taking place during the 9th annual edition of KREMFEST! We are looking for experiential VR, 360° music videos, 6DoF interactive narratives, and audio-reactive spatial art to accompany live electronic music acts across the multi-room Kremwerk complex.
