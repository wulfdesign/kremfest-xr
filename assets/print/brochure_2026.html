🌟 Headlining Experiences (UpLiftVR)
UpLiftVR ‘Maiden Flight’ Balloon Ride Headliner
By: Larry James & Julia Jackson • Runtime: 3 min 30 sec • Format: Interactive Room-Scale VR (Meta Quest 3 or PCVR Quest Link)
Step into the headset and launch into the heavens on a balloon ride's experimental maiden flight. As you ascend above the clouds, you find yourself overlooking a wondrous, lucid dreamlike land calling out to be explored. During your descent from the clouds to the grounds below, you get the feeling that this is just the beginning of a series of grand adventures.
High Desert Eclipse Headliner
By: Larry James & Julia Jackson • Runtime: 6 min 30 sec • Format: 4K 360° Video (Meta Quest)
High Desert Eclipse is a 4K 360° documentary short filmed from a majestic hilltop overlook in the remote Oregon high desert during the Great American Eclipse of August 21, 2017. Spanning 120 seconds pre- and post-totality, the rapidly encroaching shadow of the moon turns day into twilight while the entire panoramic horizon transforms into a simultaneous 360-degree sunset and sunrise.

🎬 Official Selections
Artemis: The Free Return (WebXR Orbital Sandbox)
By: Larry James (United States) • Runtime: 17 minutes (Variable 5 minutes – 60 hours)
What does it truly feel like to leave Earth behind? Pointing toward the second star to the left, take the controls of Artemis II in Low Earth Orbit and unleash 111 seconds of thunderous Trans-Lunar Injection burn to drift across the silent cislunar void. With most of your propellant spent, the only thing standing between you and the cold oblivion of deep space are a few critical midcourse corrections to thread the needle for a gravitational slingshot around the Moon and safely return to Earth. Experience the awe of deep space as the Moon swells to fill your entire field of view—and feel your stomach drop as its colossal gravity well grabs your ship on close approach, hurtling you around the lunar far side. As Earth vanishes behind the cratered horizon and total radio blackout falls, you realize that everything humanity has ever been is hundreds of thousands of miles away... and you can only hope the math is right to sling you back toward the only home we've (no... we've...) ever known at 25,000 mph. With unscripted Newtonian physics, final trajectory adjustments, and active aerodynamic lift-banking standing between you and disaster, you must thread a razor-thin 2-degree reentry keyhole through scorching atmospheric plasma. Far more than just a simulation, it becomes an awe-inspiring, heart-pounding WebXR journey among the stars.
Trip the Light: Let's Dance
By: Patrick Ascolese (Seattle, WA • USA) • Runtime: Variable (Interactive Room-Scale Rhythm Experience)
Turn your home into the hottest dance floor. Learn real moves, tear it up with a virtual partner, or crush the hottest choreos in Rhythm Challenge! Fresh off its official launch, 'Trip the Light: Let's Dance' merges full-body rhythm gameplay with nightclub visual energy. Built by Seattle-based Dark Arts Software, it transforms physical movement into spatial illumination—a rhythm game, fitness app, and dance lesson all-in-one that makes every session an exhilarating performance. A perfect crossover for the Kremwerk dance floor crowd.
CONNECTOME
By: Grant Hinkson (Seattle, WA • USA) • Runtime: Variable (Room-Scale Spatial Meditation & Art Puzzle)
Lose yourself in CONNECTOME, a space of discovery and exploration. Simple gestures bring objects to life and grow a contemplative world around you in this room-scale, interactive art and puzzle experience. Allow yourself to slow down and find flow in a place of wonder and scale only possible in VR. Optimized from the ground up for pure optical hand-tracking, CONNECTOME invites players to interact with ethereal structures using natural hand gestures. Created by Seattle-based Parietal Lab, it balances calm intellectual engagement with deep sensory tranquility.
CONSTELLATIONS: Touch the Stars
By: Grant Hinkson (Seattle, WA • USA) • Runtime: Variable (Celestial Star-Tracing & Ambient Starfield)
In CONSTELLATIONS: Touch the Stars, the celestial sphere comes to life through intuitive spatial interaction. Scan the heavens with a heads-up display, tap your thumb to open the constellation in view, and use pinch-to-drag hand gestures to connect stellar coordinates across all 88 official IAU astronomical constellations. Designed for pure optical hand tracking, CONSTELLATIONS transforms cosmic geometry into an intimate, tactile art form. Whether learning classical mythology among the stars or relaxing inside a luminous, ambient starfield, attendees experience the majesty of the night sky without physical controllers.
Project Hydra: Iterations
By: Grant Hinkson (Seattle, WA • USA) • Runtime: 13 minutes (Complete Generative Audio Loop)
Created as a virtual reality companion to the physical art installation showcased at the Seattle Coliseum of Art, Project Hydra: Iterations translates generative algorithmic systems—first rendered via physical pen plotters, projected light, and holographic displays—into towering spatial dimensions. Attendees journey through architectural chambers to confront the centerpiece '7×7: Complete Cycle', a multi-story generative sound sculpture that visits every node across a 13-minute hypnotic progression. Ambient audio responds dynamically as the focal node animates across a fixed grid of rings, offering an immersive meditation on geometry, time, and scale.
djay
By: Algoriddim (Germany (Munich) / Global) • Runtime: Interactive / Open-Ended (5:00–10:00 Recommended Turn)
Experience DJing like never before with the award-winning djay app for Meta Quest. Mix music on hyper-realistic 3D turntables, control audio effects and loops with intuitive optical hand gestures, and immerse yourself in dynamic environments that react to your mix in real time — all while keeping full visibility of Kremwerk's dancing crowd and laser lights through high-fidelity passthrough. Featuring seamless integration with streaming services and a rich library of music, djay turns anyone into an instant vinyl selector on the festival floor.
Firedrake VR
By: Jon Stone (United States) • Runtime: 8:00
Enter the Wizard’s Warren and experience a dungeon unlike any other. This one-level demo focuses on the heart of Firedrake: exploration. Wander through a handcrafted fantasy dungeon where every passage, chamber, artifact, and forgotten corner invites you to look closer. Search for hidden areas, discover mysterious objects, uncover fragments of an ancient story, and see what secrets the dungeon has been keeping. Firedrake is more than a place to fight monsters. It is a dungeon you can physically step into and explore at your own pace. Open doors, investigate strange rooms, find magical artifacts, and venture deeper into a world shaped by forgotten magic. This demo offers a small glimpse of the larger adventure Firedrake: Wizard’s Warren and its living, mysterious dungeon.
We Are Dead Animals
By: Tote Tiere Maarten (Maarten Isaäk de Heer) (Germany, Netherlands) • Runtime: 17:00 (15:00–19:00 Variable)
We Are Dead Animals is a casually transcendental VR game. Bring animals back to life in the virtual paradise created by Tote Tiere Maarten. He collected all the animals he found in his direct surroundings and placed their corpses in paradise — from a deer shot by hunters to a hedgehog run over by a car — for you to explore, collect and create new animals. … and don’t forget to eat, or you will die! EXPLORE! COLLECT! CREATE! Variable Runtime Details: If you don't eat in the game, your lifespan will be 15 minutes. By eating, you can prolong your life up to 25 minutes. Like in real life. And in the end you will die.
