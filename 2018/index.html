📜 INAUGURAL LAUNCH • OCTOBER 2018

KremFest XR 2018 Inaugural

Sponsored by Vuze Cameras, ImmersiveSquare, and Wondertek Labs, VRMakerDome and UpLiftVR Studios introduced the world to VR inside the club atmosphere of KremFest 2018.

"One of the most powerful experiences in the [SIFF] VR-Zone isn’t a film but a piece of whimsical semi-interactive entertainment."
— The Seattle Times (Maiden Flight Balloon Ride)

🌟 Inaugural Headliners (UpLiftVR Studios)

The physical balloon basket installation and Oregon eclipse documentary
🌟 Headliner

UpLiftVR ‘Maiden Flight’ Balloon Ride

By Larry James & Julia Jackson (USA)
Runtime: 3:30 min
Format: Room Scale VR (HTC Vive)
Genre: Interactive VR Experience
Comfort: Moderate

The acclaimed room-scale VR experience that brought festival-goers to tears of joy dancing inside virtual clouds as they ascended into the heavens.

USA
🌟 Headliner

High Desert Eclipse

By Larry James & Julia Jackson (USA)
Runtime: 6:30 min
Format: 4K 360° Video
Genre: 360° Documentary Short
Comfort: Comfortable

Filmed in the Oregon high desert during the Great American Eclipse of August 21, 2017.

USA
